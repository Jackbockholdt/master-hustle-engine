### 🏛️ The 4-Pillar Software Architecture

#### 1. Automated Lead Capture & Missed-Call Recovery
* **The Problem:** 62%+ of inbound customer calls to local service businesses go unanswered, losing thousands in high-intent revenue.
* **The Solution:** Instant `<15s` AI SMS text-back and conversational voice bot that pre-qualifies customer requests and schedules jobs directly into calendar software.
* **Compatibility:** Plugs into any existing carrier, PBX, or Twilio/Vapi SIP trunk.

#### 2. Custom-Branded Client Portal
* **The Problem:** Clients churn when agency reporting is manual, delayed, or invisible.
* **The Solution:** A modern obsidian-styled web application hosted on your agency's custom subdomain (`portal.youragency.com`).
* **Features:** Live lead pipeline tracker, conversion analytics, recording playback, and isolated multi-client role permissions.

#### 3. Multi-Channel Review & Reputation Engine
* **The Problem:** Stagnant Google review counts suppress local search pack rankings.
* **The Solution:** Automated post-service review request drip sequences via SMS and email.
* **Features:** Routes 5-star ratings directly to Google Business Profile & Facebook; intercepts negative feedback privately before public posting.

#### 4. Turnkey Billing & Stripe Monetization
* **The Problem:** Agencies struggle to scale recurring revenue without adding labor costs.
* **The Solution:** Collect **$299–$499/month per client** with automated Stripe Connect subscriptions and cryptographically signed license keys.
* **Economics:** ~88.4% gross software profit margins built on lightweight 3-tier token reduction architecture.
