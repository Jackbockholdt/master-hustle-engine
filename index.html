02 / RESILIENCE ARCHITECTURE

Multi-LLM Failover Router & Guardrail Pipeline

Agency client bots fail when single-provider outages or dirty queue data cause silent downtime or domain burn. Our engine enforces zero-downtime availability and safe outbound execution:

LAYER 1 · ZERO-DOWNTIME FAILOVER ROUTER
Gemini 3.6 Flash → Claude 3.5 Sonnet → OpenAI GPT-4o

Monitors upstream health continuously. If the primary provider returns HTTP 503, 429, or times out, execution automatically fails over to the hot standby provider within milliseconds.

▼
LAYER 2 · 10-POINT QUEUE & DELIVERY SCRUBBER
RFC 5322 Syntax · In-Batch Deduplication · DNC Suppression · Role & Freemail Stripping

Incoming recipient batches are scrubbed deterministically before dispatch. Blocks malformed addresses, duplicates, generic info@ accounts, consumer freemail, and active opt-outs.

▼
LAYER 3 · 48-HOUR RECIPIENT QUIET WINDOW
Deterministic Dispatch Throttle · Domain Reputation Protection

Enforces a mandatory 48-hour gap between communications to any single contact or organization. Prevents duplicate follow-ups, recipient harassment, and sender domain flagging.
