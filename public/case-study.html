Alpheynia Web Studio: 7-Day Targeted Outreach Run

A breakdown of how Master Hustle Engine set up and executed a hyper-local outbound sprint targeting service businesses across Texas and Oklahoma with an irresistible, value-first offer.

1

Client Profile: Who He Is

Talha Kamdar operates Alpheynia, a boutique web design and development studio focused on building simple, fast, and modern websites for local trade businesses, home service companies, and independent gyms.

Like many specialized design studios, Talha delivers high-end craft but faced the common bottleneck of manual prospect hunting. High-ticket web revamps require building trust upfront, where cold messages asking for phone calls or pitch meetings are routinely ignored by busy local business owners.

Client Talha Kamdar
Agency / Studio Alpheynia
Core Specialization Web Design & Development for Local Businesses
Target Market Plumbers & Fitness Studios (Austin, OKC, Tulsa)
2

The Strategy: What We Did

To generate warm, qualified sales conversations for Talha without sounding like a generic offshore email blast, Master Hustle Engine structured a 7-day outbound test around authentic local research and upfront demonstration of value.

  • 🔍
    Targeted 50 Verified High-Intent Businesses

    Curated a tight list of 50 established plumbers and independent gyms across Austin TX, Oklahoma City OK, and Tulsa OK that had no website, a broken page, or an outdated layout.

  • 🎨
    Created a Value-First "Free Homepage Mockup" Pitch

    Rather than pitching a sales call, Talha offered to build each prospect a free, tailored sample homepage mockup with zero charge and no strings attached. If they liked it, they could discuss a full site; if not, they kept the design anyway.

  • 📧
    Engineered a 3-Touch Follow-Up Cadence

    Designed a concise, human sequence consisting of Day 1 customized outreach, a gentle 48-hour follow-up bump, and a professional breakup note to maximize response rates without burning goodwill.

  • ✅
    Enforced Real-Time Deliverability and Domain Safety

    Pre-screened every target domain for live DNS MX records, verified on-site contact authenticity, and applied automated bounce suppression to keep sending domains spotless.
